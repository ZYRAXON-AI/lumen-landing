FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does setup really take?

Most teams are live in under an afternoon. Connect your tools with one click, and Lumen maps your categories automatically. If you'd rather have help, our onboarding is included on Growth and above.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. Lumen is SOC 2 Type II certified, uses read-only connections by default, encrypts everything at rest and in transit, and never sells or trains on your data. You can delete your workspace and all records at any time.

Which tools do you integrate with?

Stripe, Shopify, Xero, QuickBooks, Google Ads, Meta Ads, HubSpot, Salesforce, and 40+ more through our API. There's a webhook receiver if you need something custom.

What happens after the trial?

Nothing charges. If you don't upgrade within 14 days you move to the free Starter plan — your history stays intact and you can export it as CSV or PDF at any point.

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. Cancel in two clicks from settings, no retention call, no exit fee. You keep access until the end of the billing period.
